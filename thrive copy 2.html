Our Marketing Process

6 strategic steps to help brands grow and thrive in the market.

Step 1

Research

  • We start by gathering insights about the marketing problem through comprehensive brand audits, client interviews and benchmark analysis

Step 2

Marketing Strategy

  • With these insights and the client’s preferences, we develop an annual strategy with several tactics such as experiential, media buy or influencer campaigns

Step 3

Creative Ideation

  • We then bring our marketing strategy to life through brand mockups to vividly envision where the brand will be, say in a space of a year

Step 4

Production

  • We then convert our creative ideas into tangible materials e.g., actual social media posts or pull up banners

Step 5

Execution

  • We then continuously publish our marketing materials based on strategy for example through continuous social media posts or Ads

Step 6

M&E

  • To determine whether we are achieving our objectives and the client's targets, we constantly assess ourselves through monthly and annual reports.
